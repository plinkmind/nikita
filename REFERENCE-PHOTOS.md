# Reference photos that would help (please send what you can)

I cannot see your phone, so real photos and screen recordings are the fastest way to get the results right. Nothing below is required to
use the site. Do not send photos of other people without their permission. You can blur your face if you prefer.

## First, for every problem: a screen recording or screenshot with the debug view
Open the site with `?debug` at the end of the address (for example `https://glamify-xr.netlify.app/?debug`), press **Try It On**, apply the item that goes wrong, and record 10 to 15 seconds of your screen while you do the movement that breaks it (turn left, turn right, look up, look down, move closer). The green dots show what the camera model sees, and the text shows dropouts and glitches. Please also tell me: **phone model, browser (Chrome/Safari), and whether it was the front camera.**

## Earrings and jewellery
| Send | Why |
|---|---|
| The `?debug` recording above, showing an earring in the wrong place | Shows me the exact head angle where it fails |
| **Transparent PNG of each real earring / necklace / nose ring**: front view, high resolution (at least 1000 px tall), no background, plus its real size in millimetres | Today jewellery is drawn with simple shapes. A real product picture will look far more realistic |
| One **front photo and one three-quarter photo** of a person wearing earrings like yours (close-up on the ear) | To check size, hang length and how the earring sits against the ear |

## Makeup
| Send | Why |
|---|---|
| The `?debug` recording of the flicker (turning your head slowly with a lipstick applied) | The debug numbers show whether the camera model drops the face (dropouts) or sends wrong readings (glitches) |
| A close-up photo of the lip colour you want to match (a real swatch on lips) | To check the colour and shine look right |

## Hair
| Send | Why |
|---|---|
| **Photos of the hairstyles you want to offer** on real people: front, three-quarter and side, plain background, hair clearly visible | To decide the style list for the AI hairstyle mode, and to compare AI results against |
| A front photo of yourself (plain wall, even light, hair visible, no hat), to test the AI photo mode | The AI mode works from a clear photo, not the live camera |
| Tell me: is a live hairstyle preview essential? | A live preview of a *cut* cannot look real. If it is essential, the only way is **professionally made transparent hair pieces** (front, side, back) from a 3D artist, which is a separate piece of work |

## Outfits
| Send | Why |
|---|---|
| **Product photos of 5 to 10 real garments**: front view, flat-lay or ghost mannequin, plain white or plain colour background, at least 1000 px tall, JPG or PNG | These are the garment inputs the AI model needs, and what the live "My clothing photo" mode uses |
| **2 to 3 photos of a person**, standing straight, upper body or full body, facing the camera, arms slightly away from the body, even light, plain background | To test the AI outfit try-on on a good input |
| **1 to 2 screenshots of a try-on result you consider realistic** (from any website or app) | So I know what quality target you mean. I will use it to judge, not to copy |

## Nails
| Send | Why |
|---|---|
| **A close-up photo of the back of a hand**, fingers slightly apart, bright even light, nails clearly visible | To compare my nail shape and size to a real hand |
| A photo of the finished nail look you want (each shape: square, round, almond, coffin, stiletto, and the French / glitter designs) | To match the shapes and finishes |
| The `?debug` recording of the nails going wrong, showing the blue hand dots | Shows whether the hand model or my nail drawing is at fault |

## Format notes
- Product images: transparent PNG for jewellery, plain-background JPG or PNG for garments.
- Larger is better (1000 to 3000 px). Do not add watermarks.
- Please only send images you have the right to use.
